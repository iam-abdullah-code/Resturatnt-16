About Us

Discover the story behind Pakistan's most loved restaurant brand

Our Story

Salt'n Pepper Restaurant

The Salt’n Pepper is Pakistan’s most recognized and distinguished hospitality brand with a history of market innovation and excellence in restaurants chain operation. The Salt’n Pepper Restaurants are regarded and respected as the number one restaurant entity in Pakistan by local and foreign observers.

Established 1983

The first Salt’n Pepper Restaurant on the Lahore Mall Road was established in 1983 by hotelier and restaurateur Mahmood Akbar, considered by many as the pioneer hotelier and restaurateur in Pakistan.

Foodconsults (Pvt.) Ltd.

Foodconsults (Pvt.) Limited, the owning company of Salt’n Pepper Restaurants, has its headquarters in Lahore and prides itself on developing Pakistan’s first national chain of restaurants.

Cuisine Excellence

For over three decades, the group’s focus has been to provide guests with an exceptional dining experience. Expertise includes Pakistani, Continental cuisine, and Fast Food.

Our Journey

Restaurant

The Salt’n Pepper Restaurants at Mall Road and Liberty Market Lahore are considered to be the first proper family style restaurants in the food industry of Pakistan. These restaurants pride themselves for being symbolized as an eatery with a customer loyalty spanning over three generations. As a restaurant model, these have inspired the set-up of numerous other restaurants across Pakistan.

Village Restaurant

The Salt’n Pepper Village Restaurant in Lahore is considered to be the restaurant that revolutionized dining experiences in Pakistan. It offers a unique buffet and à la carte experience with a seating capacity of 300.

Expansion

The brand has expanded to multiple cities including Islamabad and Faisalabad, and has also started giving franchises of its restaurants, bringing the Salt’n Pepper experience to more families across Pakistan.
